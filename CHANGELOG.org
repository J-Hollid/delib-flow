  `docs/implementation-plan.org` to reflect template-backed note creation.

  `docs/implementation-plan.org`: discovery heuristics, capture-template-backed

  `docs/implementation-plan.org` to reflect that filing conflict detection is

- Added end-to-end Milestone 2 workflow coverage in
  `test/delib-flow-test.el` for both the local matched-project path and the
  cloud-assisted approval path.
- Updated `README.org` and `docs/implementation-plan.org` to treat Milestone 2
  as a complete core workflow with remaining refinement work called out as

  `docs/implementation-plan.org` to reflect the reintegration-approval stage
